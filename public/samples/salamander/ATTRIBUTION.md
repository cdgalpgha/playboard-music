# Salamander Grand Piano

Piano samples by **Alexander Holm**, recorded from a **Yamaha C5 grand piano**.
Licensed under **Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported**:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

Source collection and MP3 conversion distributed by Tone.js:
https://github.com/Tonejs/audio/tree/master/salamander
Original project: https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano

Playboard bundles the 30 MP3 files provided by Tone.js, sampled in minor thirds
from A0 to C8. Playback transposes the nearest sample by at most one semitone.
These MP3 files do not include the original instrument's full 16 velocity layers.
Velocity gain, filtering, release envelopes, stereo placement, and room convolution
are applied during playback. No recordings have been changed on disk.

See ORIGINAL-README.txt for the source's license and technical information.
manifest.json records the SHA-256 digest and size of each bundled sample.

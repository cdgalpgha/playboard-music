# Milestone 2 report: falling notes, scoring, calibration, practice tools

## What works

- **Chart model and tempo map** (`core/chart.ts`, `core/tempo-map.ts`): notes in
  seconds and beats, measures, beat grid (compound meters count dotted
  quarters), tempo changes.
- **MusicXML parser** (`formats/musicxml.ts`, pulled forward from M3): parts and
  staves, hands from staves/parts/clefs, backup/forward, chords, ties, grace
  notes, fingering, dynamics, tempo marks, repeats with volta endings.
- **Content pipeline**: compact notation → MusicXML → parsed back and checked
  at build time; catalog refuses items without a license record. Two
  public-domain pieces in Playboard arrangements: Ode to Joy, Minuet in G.
- **Scoring engine** (`core/scoring.ts`): nearest-unmatched matching per pitch,
  perfect/good/early/late/miss windows (40/90/180 ms) judged in real time,
  wider at slow tempos, wrong-note penalty, per-hand accuracy, timing
  histogram in 10 ms bins, mean and spread, velocity consistency, three
  weakest measures with a one-click loop.
- **Transport** (`core/transport.ts`): tempo 25–150%, seamless loops on a
  monotonic time axis, wait-for-me holds.
- **Practice session** (`practice/session.ts`): 150 ms look-ahead scheduling of
  auto-played notes and clicks on the audio clock, one-bar count-in,
  hands separate with the other hand auto-played (or silent), Listen mode,
  metronome on the chart's beats, wait-for-me, loop passes with their own
  results, sessions saved to IndexedDB.
- **Falling-notes renderer** (`render/falling-notes.ts`): Canvas 2D, lanes
  aligned to the on-screen keyboard, hand colours, finger numbers,
  measure/beat lines, judgement outlines and hit flashes, wait highlight;
  reduced-motion users get static feedback without pulses or bursts.
- **Calibration**: two tap-along passes (clicks, then flashes), median with
  MAD outlier rejection, input and audio offsets saved per device pair, used
  by scoring (input) and scheduling (audio).

## Measured numbers

Headless Chromium 141, Linux container, software rendering.

| What | Result |
|---|---|
| Falling notes, Minuet in G playing | 60.0 fps mean, p95 frame 16.7 ms, 0 dropped frames |
| Canvas draw cost per frame | p95 0.2 ms (1.2% of a 60 Hz frame) |
| Scoring from MIDI timestamps | notes stamped +5, +70, +130, −150 ms → perfect, good, late, early (e2e) |
| Calibration with a simulated player (10 ms visual, 25 ms audio habit) | input offset 12 ms, audio offset 14 ms (expected 10 and 15; ~2 ms test-harness delay) |

## Tests

- Vitest: 75 unit tests (adds tempo map, MusicXML writer/parser round trip and
  edge cases, scoring, transport and loops, wait gate, calibration math).
- Playwright: 10 end-to-end tests (adds timestamp-based scoring, wait-for-me,
  loop + tempo + hands, calibration flow, renderer frame rate).

## Known issues

- The frame-rate figure is from a headless container, not a mid-range
  laptop. The draw cost (0.2 ms) leaves wide headroom, and the practice stage
  shows live fps so it can be checked on real hardware.
- Pieces whose range exceeds a small keyboard show a warning; there is no
  automatic octave shifting yet.
- Changing tempo or hands while playing restarts the run's scoring (by design,
  D20), which can surprise someone who expects a running total.
- D.C./D.S. jumps are not expanded (D21).

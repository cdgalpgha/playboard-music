# M8 report: beginner-first redesign, chord mode, YouTube play-along

Follow-up to user feedback after M7: the computer-key chord mode from
Playboard v0.2 was missing, YouTube play-along didn't work without an API key,
and the app should guide a complete beginner.

## What changed

- **Start here** is the landing page. Four steps for a first session, each
  ticked off from real state: sound on, first note played, a lesson started,
  a video opened. The next step is highlighted with its one button.
- **Menu** uses the v0.2 names: Play studio, Learn to play, Chord book, Song
  library, Play along, My progress. "Connect a keyboard", Settings and About
  sit under "More".
- **Chord mode** (default in Play studio and Play along): A–G major, Shift
  minor, Space sharp, Z/X octave, triads or sevenths, transpose −6…+6. Piano
  mode keeps the chromatic layout with Space as sustain.
- **Play studio**: piano first, chord controls under it, then the practice
  room and a "paste a YouTube link" card. Sound sliders, metronome and
  recording are folded into "Sound, metronome and recording". Pedal noise,
  latency and engine details are under "For advanced players".
- **Practice room** (from v0.2): Pop, R&B, Jazz, Rock, Classical and Lo-fi
  four-chord loops with soft backing chords and a click. A chord played within
  ±220 ms of the bar line counts as a well-timed bar. A wrong chord gets a hint
  naming the right one. Any input counts: MIDI, computer keys or on-screen keys.
- **Chord book** (from v0.2): every chord in 8 types and 3 shapes, filtered to
  major and minor by default, with a drawn keyboard and tap-to-hear.
- **Play along**: paste any YouTube link (no API key needed). The video plays
  in YouTube's official player. The title and uploader come from the player,
  with a "Watch on YouTube" link. Play/pause, seek, speed, video volume, an A–B
  loop and recent sessions. The piano with chord keys sits underneath. Search
  appears when a Data API key is set.

## Tests

- Unit: 131 passed. New tests cover chord building, voicings, transpose,
  inversions, chord recognition and bar timing.
- End-to-end: `tests/studio.spec.ts` adds four specs:
  - The Start here guide ticks off steps.
  - Play along: a pasted link opens the player with the uploader credited, the A–B loop jumps back, Shift+A plays Am, and the video appears in recent sessions.
  - The Chord book filters and plays.
  - The practice room scores an on-beat G and coaches a wrong chord.

  The practice-room test presses the key from inside the page when the bar
  highlights. Pressing it from the test runner added 250–490 ms of polling
  delay and failed half the time.

## Not verified here
